# Privacy Policy

**Last.fm Scrobbler for YouTube™ — v4.0.0**

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Last.fm Scrobbler for YouTube™ is an independently maintained browser extension that detects music being played on YouTube and YouTube Music and submits scrobbling information to Last.fm.

## Information the Extension Processes

The extension processes information needed to identify and scrobble the currently playing track, including:

- Artist name
- Track title
- Track duration
- Playback position
- YouTube/YouTube Music video information
- Last.fm account/session information required for scrobbling

This information is processed by the extension to provide its scrobbling functionality.

## Last.fm Credentials

The extension allows users to enter their Last.fm API key and API secret.

These credentials are stored locally using the browser's extension storage and are not sent to the extension developer or to any developer-operated server.

Last.fm API requests are made directly to Last.fm's services.

The extension also stores the Last.fm authorization session locally so that users can remain connected without repeatedly authorizing the extension.

## Information Sent to Third Parties

The extension sends scrobbling information to Last.fm because this is required for the extension's primary purpose.

The extension does not operate its own server or backend for collecting user information.

The extension does not sell user information or provide user information to advertisers.

Last.fm may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms. The extension developer does not control Last.fm's handling of information after it is transmitted to Last.fm.

## YouTube and YouTube Music

The extension reads information from YouTube and YouTube Music pages in order to identify the currently playing music.

This information is used for the extension's scrobbling functionality and is not sent to the extension developer's own servers.

## Local Storage

The extension uses the browser's local extension storage to save settings and Last.fm connection information.

Users can clear the extension's stored information through the extension's settings or by removing the extension from their browser.

## Data Collection by the Developer

The extension developer does not operate a server that collects the information processed by the extension.

The extension does not intentionally collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data handling changes. The latest version will always be available in this repository.
